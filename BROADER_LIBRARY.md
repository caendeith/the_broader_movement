# 📚 The Broader Library

> A living catalog of works and authors in the Broader community.

This is where Broaders share what they make and what they love. Adding your work here is one way to pay the stream forward.

---

## 🔁 How it works

- **List honestly** — put the *official* store link next to every work, so anyone who can afford it, buys it.
- **Broad with attribution** — always credit the author.
- **Respect the Broader License** — [`BROADER_LICENSE.md`](BROADER_LICENSE.md).

---

## ➕ How to add your work

1. **Fork** this repository.
2. **Add a row** to the *Authors* table (if you're new) and/or the *Works* table below.
3. **Open a Pull Request** with a short note.

Copy-paste these templates and replace the placeholders:

**Author row**
```
| `@handle` | What you make | [official](https://) | [support](https://) |
```

**Work row**
```
| *Title* | `@handle` | Type | Year | [buy](https://) | Note |
```
